The Four Policy Pillars
01
Code of Professional Ethics

Sets the moral foundation for how every staff member conducts themselves inside and outside school premises.

  • Honesty and transparency in all professional dealings
  • Respectful, non-discriminatory conduct toward all students
  • Avoidance of conflict of interest and favoritism
  • Confidentiality of student and school information
02
Dress & Appearance Standards

Professional appearance guidelines that reinforce the school's values and serve as a visible model of excellence for students.

  • Business-appropriate attire for all teaching staff
  • ID badge worn visibly at all times during school hours
  • Formal wear on official school events and ceremonies
  • Grooming standards that reflect a professional image
03
Digital & Social Media Policy

Responsible digital conduct guidelines that protect students, uphold the school's reputation, and maintain professional boundaries online.

  • No sharing of student data or images without consent
  • No personal social media connections with students
  • Professional tone in all digital communications
  • School equipment used for educational purposes only
04
Performance & Accountability

A fair accountability framework that ensures every staff member is supported in growth while held to clear professional standards.

  • Bi-annual performance appraisals via RPMS framework
  • Mandatory 20 hours professional development per year
  • Clear 3-tier disciplinary process for violations
  • Recognition program for exemplary professional conduct
